# Plate Planner — Elevator Pitch  *(simple, attention-grabbing version)*

**4 speakers &nbsp;•&nbsp; ~22 seconds each &nbsp;•&nbsp; ~1 min 25 sec total**

The goal is simple: make the audience *want* the demo. Almost no jargon. One image, one promise, one ask.

---

## 🎤 Speaker 1 — Sandilya  *(the hook)*  ~20 sec

> "Quick question — how many of you have stood in front of an open fridge with no idea what to cook?
>
> *(pause, half-smile, scan the room)*
>
> Yeah. All of us.
>
> The average household loses **three to five hours a week** on that exact decision. That's a part-time job in fridge-staring."

*Delivery: warm and playful. The pause after the question is the whole moment — let people actually react. The "part-time job in fridge-staring" line should get a chuckle.*

---

## 🎤 Speaker 2 — Sai Priyanka  *(the pain)*  ~18 sec

> "And recipe apps don't really fix it.
>
> They show you twenty beautiful dishes you **can't actually make**, because you're missing one ingredient. Or two. So you scroll. You sigh. You order takeout.
>
> Again."

*Delivery: a little wry. The three short sentences — "scroll, sigh, takeout" — are a rhythm; let each one land. The single word "Again." is the punchline; pause before it.*

---

## 🎤 Speaker 3 — Pavan Charith  *(the magic)*  ~22 sec

> "That's where **Plate Planner** comes in.
>
> Tell it what's in your pantry — butter, eggs, flour, whatever you've got — and it instantly hands you recipes you can actually cook **tonight**.
>
> Missing something? It tells you the perfect swap. *Out of butter for cookies? Use margarine.*  Done. Dinner solved."

*Delivery: this is the energy lift of the pitch. Talk faster, smile, gesture. "Done. Dinner solved." should sound effortless — like a magic trick that just worked.*

---

## 🎤 Speaker 4 — Sai Dheeraj  *(the close + ask)*  ~22 sec

> "What makes Plate Planner different is that it actually **understands** ingredients — how they pair, how they swap, what works for *baking* vs. what works for *sautéing*.
>
> Less staring. Less wasted food. Less takeout.
>
> Whether you're a busy student, a tired parent, or just trying to use up that cilantro before it goes bad — **come try our demo.**
>
> Plate Planner: turn what's already in your kitchen into dinner."

*Delivery: bring the energy back up. The three-beat "Less staring. Less wasted food. Less takeout." is a rhythmic close — slow it down a touch. Make eye contact when you say "come try our demo." End with the tagline, then a beat of silence and a smile.*

## 🎯 The Three Lines That Have to Land

1. **"A part-time job in fridge-staring."** — earns the laugh and the attention.
2. **"Scroll. Sigh. Takeout. Again."** — names the pain in five words.
3. **"Plate Planner: turn what's already in your kitchen into dinner."** — the tagline they should walk away repeating.

Everything else is connective tissue. If anything has to be cut for time, cut the connective tissue, not these three lines.

---

## 🎬 Quick Delivery Reminders

- **Stand in a tight semicircle** — feels like a team, not a queue.
- **Don't memorise word-for-word.** Lock in the bolded phrases; let the rest stay conversational.
- **Hand off with eye contact.** When you finish, glance at the next speaker for a beat — they pick up immediately.
- **Smile at the close.** This is a project about food. People should feel like coming over to the booth would be fun.
